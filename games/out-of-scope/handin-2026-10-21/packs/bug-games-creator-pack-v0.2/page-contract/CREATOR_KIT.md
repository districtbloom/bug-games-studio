# Creator kit: what you hand in, and what bug.games does without it

**For creators publishing on bug.games, and for the Studio team that builds the upload slots.**

The rule: **a game with no art still works.** The platform always has a fallback, so nothing about the flow, the numbers or the money changes when an asset is missing. Art makes the game look like itself; the kit says which art matters first.

Labels: **PROVEN** = rendered on the boards for at least two worlds · **PROPOSED** = not rendered, or one example only. The fallbacks for the return path (Continue, rail, rewards, seals, result) were OBSERVED on the platform's return-path board ("No art provided"). Fallbacks on every other surface are extended here and are PROPOSED.

## Three tiers

| Tier | What you supply | What players get |
|---|---|---|
| **0 · Nothing** | A title, a kind, one line | A working game everywhere, in a plain catalogue look generated by the platform. No reactions. Not eligible for the For You wall |
| **1 · Minimum** (target for every published game) | 9 assets + 4 words (below) | Your world on every surface; the small marks hold up at real size |
| **2 · Full** (the 13 roster worlds, partners) | Tier 1 + scene module, page premise kit, event and update art, share card | The living poster on For You and a game page that is entirely your world |

## Self-check in Studio (run before publishing)

Each line is a check Studio should run or show. None of these exist in Studio yet: run them yourself, and run `selfcheck/validate.py` on your package.

1. **Squint test**: the poster at 90×70. Can you still name the game?
2. **Caption strip**: nothing essential in the bottom 26 px of any crop.
3. **Lettering**: none on any plate that can squeeze below ~200 px; none smaller than ~12 px letter height.
4. **Light map**: run `tools/measure.py light` on your 2:1 still and read the band. Bands: dark L < .20, mid .20–.50, light ≥ .50, loud C ≥ .45. Then check every wall edge: no light–light, no loud–loud, and mid–mid only with ΔL ≥ .10.
5. **Platform colours**: run `tools/measure.py colour '#RRGGBB'` on every palette hex (always with the `#`).
   - **Guarded** (ΔE2000 < 12 to ember, lilac or sun): never a button-like fill (a rectangle or pill ≥ 24×24 px, or one with upright text or a number), and never within 120 px of Play, a price or a selected state.
   - **Caution** (12–20): the 120 px rule only.
6. **Small marks**: icon 46 and emblem 30 at 1× on night and on linen, beside three other games' marks. Does yours read as a different object, and not as a blob, a gem, wifi or a letter?
7. **Seals**: all of them in a 32 px strip. Are the three kind outlines distinguishable, and is locked a quiet "not yet"?
8. **Numbers in art**: none in the key still, result still or reward tiles.
9. **Reward verb**: does it collide with an existing world's verb (stamp, brackets, silk, scope creep, number called, lights, squash, lift, splat, stet, found, rings, unpick)?
10. **Reduced motion**: every animated asset has a held frame that is a print worth keeping.
11. **Words**: title ≤40, place ≤30, one verb; no "teardown", "Fair Play", streak or scarcity words.
12. **Fallback preview**: see your game as tier 0. Is anything you supplied worse than the fallback?

## Bible: required sections

A tier-2 world hands in a bible: a `BIBLE.md` plus a board `render/Bible<Key>.dc.html` and `assets/*.png`. These are the sections every one of the 13 roster bibles has, so a reviewer can tell when a bible is complete. Each claim in it carries a label: DERIVED (from the module, sketches or catalogue), PROPOSED (new), OBSERVED (seen in a render).

| # | Section | Must contain |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Emotional character | Three words and one sentence |
| 2 | Defining subjects | Always / allowed / never lists; the one image |
| 3 | Palette | Hex · role · share at rest (measured) · meaning. The **collision rule** with ΔE to ember, lilac and sun (self-check 5). Light-map L and C (self-check 4) |
| 4 | Typography | Game lettering with minimum sizes and where it may appear; the rule beside Bricolage and DM Mono |
| 5 | Materials and marks | Drawing rules (line, fill, light, texture) |
| 6 | Composition and framing | Focal path; 2:1, 2:3, 1:1 and 9:16 crops (what moves in narrow crops); bottom 26 px; share-card layout |
| 7 | Motion | Idle, reaction, enter, loop, squeezed; the **reward verb** in 3 frames; what never moves; the reduced-motion still |
| 8 | Scale behaviour | Drop order, **last survivors**, icon 46 / emblem 30 / chip 64 drawn separately and checked at 1× |
| 9 | Supporting assets | Store (6), pass (name, unit, row grounds, 8 steps), seals (8, outline by kind, three states, checked at 32 px), event banner, update card, Continue key still, result still, share card |
| 10 | Voice | Verb, rants and hits, yes/no lines |
| 11 | DO / DON'T | Drawn pairs |
| 12 | Page premise | Carrying idea, section surface + plate, five tab objects, owned mark, reward, floor, kept chrome |
| 13 | How this differs from <nearest roster game> | A table on space, light, palette, line, edge, motion, reward, lettering, page |
| 14 | Verification record | What was rendered and looked at, console errors, `check.py`, small marks at 1×, known weak spots |


## Tooling notes (for anyone rendering boards)

- `living-poster/tools/shot.mjs` serves a `render/` folder (`--root`). `living-poster/harness.py` copies `support.js` beside the board it writes.
- The fonts the boards ask for (Bricolage Grotesque, DM Mono, Geist Mono, Instrument Serif/Sans) are in `living-poster/tools/fonts/` and served locally, so no network is needed. Any other face your world draws text in must be added there (and to the list in `tools/pw.mjs`), or it falls back silently.
- Image readers shrink very tall PNGs until they are unreadable. Read boards in slices of about 1440×1300.
