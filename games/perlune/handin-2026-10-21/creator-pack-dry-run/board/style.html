Perlune · style sheet

bug.games creator pack v0.2 · stage 1 of 3 · dry run · 19 Oct 2026

Perlune looks like glossy sculpture floating in a neon-lined night: a droplet of liquid metal, glass and magnetic dust with two big eyes, tiny ringed worlds, and the Eclipse at the centre of everything. Everything in the game is drawn by its own WebGL renderer; this sheet is read from that renderer's key art, cover and frames, and every rule below is one the renderer already follows.

Reference frames from the game's own key art, cover and play

2 · Cover, with lettering. The title, the three-lineage eyebrow, the tagline, the iridescent button shape.
3 · Key art 9:16. The same image stacked: Eclipse above, droplet below, horizon at the foot.

The rules of the look

Line

  • No ink outlines, ever. Forms are made by light: a bright thin-film rim (fresnel) and a silhouette against night.
  • Lines exist only as light: neon contour lines in the sky (thin, glowing, pink / cyan / pearl), contour bands on planets, ribbons, orbit hairlines, sunburst rays.

Fill

  • Glossy materials, never flat: liquid pearl-chrome (the droplet), pearl, clear glass, obsidian with a coloured rim, pure emissive glow.
  • Every shiny surface mirrors one procedural "studio": a dark violet sky, a cyan band, a pale horizon line. Planets: banded fills that follow the terrain.

Texture

  • Smooth gradients, fine film grain, star specks and dust motes.
  • Nothing else: no paper, hatching, crayon, pixels, halftone or photo texture.

Lighting

  • Key light from the upper left, behind the viewer; wrap light on planets.
  • Bloom on emissive parts only (the halo, eye catchlights, the Eclipse rim, rays); horizontal anamorphic streaks on the hottest highlights.
  • The miniature lens: subject sharp, near and far soft (tilt-shift or round depth of field).

Subject and framing

  • The droplet is always the subject: one round body, two big dark oval eyes with one white catchlight each. Only the droplet has a face.
  • The one image: the Eclipse behind its head like a halo. Worlds are tiny: a horizon curve is always in reach.
  • Night everywhere, never black-black: void #05040A to nebula #2A0B4D.

Lettering

  • "PERLUNE" in heavy geometric capitals, tracked +4 %, filled with the iridescent sweep pink → cyan → cream → lilac, with a small cyan / magenta split and a soft violet glow.
  • Eyebrow: mono capitals, wide tracking, in the three lineage colours. Tagline: light geometric capitals, wide tracking, warm white.
  • Title lettering only where allowed (cover, share card, lockup), never on a plate that can shrink below ~200 px.

What never appears

  • Outlines, flat cartoon fills, pixel art, paper or crayon textures, photographs.
  • A face on anything but the droplet; enemies are faceless sculptures with one hostile glow.
  • Text, numbers or prices in stills, seals, tiles or rewards; any HUD or menu chrome.
  • Halo gold, eclipse gold or resonance violet as a flat, button-like fill, or within 120 px of Play, a price or a selected state (tiles, rewards, marker and small marks use the near-UI set).
  • Gold as "earned" on a seal (the platform's sun says earned).
  • Rounded frames (the platform draws sharp corners); fog or glow over everything; gore.
  • Another game's art, characters, names or words.
